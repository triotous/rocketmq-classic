Private failure-injection seams must not enter the public API.

Lifecycle implementation: `3c1ff59`. Registration APIs are async Rust representations of Java's synchronized mutation/wire-cleanup order, not new configuration/features. A single owned control worker holds a weak idle runtime reference. Pending registration delivery rolls back by identity when the receiving future is cancelled; accepted tokens remain explicitly unregistered. Wire unregister finishes even if a caller cancels its wait, and replacement registration cannot overtake it. The internal producer group remains in heartbeat snapshots after start, and normal stop unregisters it before transport closure. Additional runtime Arc owners do not prevent last-registration stop. Failed startup clears unusable registrations, closes transport, joins control and identity-removes the manager entry; old holders retain Failed state. Two startup boundaries are tested so far, not all P6-V03 boundaries.
